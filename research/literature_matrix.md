## Closest studies and precise differences from the proposed question

**C5 and C10 are closest to noise mismatch.** C5 directly studies noise transfer across ECG denoisers; C10 explicitly holds out noise domains. A new paper cannot claim that unseen-noise evaluation itself is new. Our provisional question instead fixes synthetic clean-signal families and power-SNR while changing noise process, then compares frozen classical tuning against one fixed compact architecture. Full C5 methods are still needed to establish exactly what its protocol lacks. [C5](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-981-99-8388-9_40), [C10](https://doi.org/10.3390/s26144489)

**C9 is closest to the combined benchmark concept.** It already connects synthetic noise, classical/neural processing, interpretable signal details and resources. Our possible distinction is a denoiser-only, matched-versus-shifted experiment with all models fitted under declared source conditions and executed on one CPU. This is a narrower protocol contribution, not evidence that C9 ignored robustness or fairness. [C9](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-026-36656-2)

**C7, C4 and P1 are closest to the efficiency/detail combination.** Our proposed novelty cannot be “lightweight denoising with morphology preservation.” The possible distinction is explicit pulse/edge errors under held-out stochastic noise processes and the inclusion of training/tuning costs in an all-CPU measurement. P1 has a convolutional-recurrent architecture and remains a preprint. [C7](https://doi.org/10.3390/s25030801), [C4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6579/ac96cd), [P1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.12478)

**C8 and C3 are closest to controlled synthetic/semi-synthetic construction.** Our entirely analytic signals provide exact targets and feature annotations but weaker application realism. C8 is especially relevant to justifying colored/impulsive components; its application-specific synthesis is not interchangeable with a generic AR(1) or Bernoulli–Gaussian noise model. [C8](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ultras.2026.108054), [C3](https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-2552/ac2bf8)

**C2 and A1 guard against loose use of “generalization.”** Reusing an architecture across datasets, testing different realizations, holding out noise types, and extrapolating noise strength are different experiments. A1 also makes architecture-dependent conclusions essential: failure of one biased CNN must not be described as failure of deep learning. [C2](https://doi.org/10.23919/Eusipco47968.2020.9287738), [A1](https://openreview.net/pdf/ae5ecd6a31912546f2851cd3d1034a3537b633b8.pdf)
